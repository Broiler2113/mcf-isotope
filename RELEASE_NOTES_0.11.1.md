# Isotope 0.11.1

## Battlefield view

- New **Heightmap** toggle (**Shift+H**, rebindable): every piece of cover shows its height as a number — 0.5, 1, 1.5 or 2. Full-height walls are drawn dimmer so low cover stands out. Under fog of war only what you can see right now is labelled.
- The Heightmap is in the battle side panel and in the map editor (top bar and View menu).
- Open doors and airlocks are see-through: the floor under them shows.
- Solar panels are even: equal cells, and both panels on a walkway match.

## Lighting

- Lamps can be dimmer or brighter as well as recoloured: right-click a lamp in the editor and pick an **Intensity** from 25 % to 300 %.
- A broken lamp scatters glass shards, just like a window.
- A lamp caught in a frag grenade's blast now rolls to survive like any other glass instead of always breaking. A wall lamp only rolls if it faces the blast.

## Combat

- Hard AI soldiers now get out of announced artillery and gas zones (and standing gas), and never walk into one.
- Gas hides what is behind it again even with fog of war off.
- Glass shows its "Glass N+" roll again for every weapon except the anti-tank shell.

## Lobby

- Deployment zones on the map preview are clearly coloured in each side's faction colour — Conclave-NOVA red, Purifiers yellow — with a thin outline.

## Multiplayer

- 0.11.1 can only play with 0.11.1: frag grenades and gas changed how a match plays out.
