# Isotope 0.11.3

## Fog of war

- Casings, glass shards, blood, laser marks and tank tracks that land out of your sight stay hidden until one of your soldiers sees the spot.
- Once seen, they stay on the map under the fog as you last saw them. Something cleaned away in the dark stays until you look again.
- Bodies follow the same rule: a soldier who falls in the dark isn't shown until you look there.
- A long laser mark only shows the part you actually saw.
- Zooming far out no longer shows explosions that happened in the dark.
- Gas clouds are hidden in any fog of war. You see a cloud only where your soldiers could see it, never behind a wall. The warning frame and its countdown still show.
- Tracer bullets only show where you can see them.
- Before / After keeps the fog: it shows the same fogged areas as the battle and never reveals where the enemy started.

## Drones

- A drone hovering over a wall can't see anything until it comes back down.

## Battle

- When raiders arrive, the AI speed setting appears and controls how fast their turn plays.

## Random maps

- The "Flammable" option is gone from Mechanics. Whether a map burns now depends on its terrain type.

## Look

- The wooden wall no longer has dark dots on its planks.

## Multiplayer

- 0.11.3 can only play with 0.11.3, because a drone over a wall now sees differently.
