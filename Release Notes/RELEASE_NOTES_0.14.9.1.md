# Isotope 0.14.9.1

- Tanks can cross and stop over trenches without destroying them or killing the units inside. Bodies and items in trenches are preserved too.
- Kamikaze and scout drone propellers now spin in the 2D view.
- Removed the blue halo and rim around the Earth in first person.
- Mines render sharply in the 2D view.
- Random event zones, including artillery barrages and incoming waves, have evenly spaced diagonal lines, stronger borders and clearer labels. The markings stay readable when zooming and through fog of war.

**Multiplayer:** all players must update to 0.14.9.1 because the tank movement rules changed (protocol 37).
