# Isotope 0.14.3

## First person in the tank's look

- Everything in first person was redrawn to match the SVAROG tank: realistic, weathered and pixelated.
- Walls show real bricks, blocks, hull panels, concrete, planks, sandbags and rock, with grime, rust streaks and scratches. Long walls no longer repeat every meter.
- The LDF wall has its own matte black panels with worn stencil marks.
- Department colors are worn paint along the walls instead of a flat band.
- Doors and airlocks, floors, ceilings, roofs, open ground and every furniture material were redrawn.
- Chunks of a destroyed wall show the wall they came from.
- Fire burns as pixel flames; smoke and gas hang as soft billows.
- Shuttles and borgs wear weathered neutral paint, with their side's color on stripes and the cab roof.

## Drones

- Kamikaze and scout drones are new low-poly models in the tank's style: a quadcopter with a strapped warhead, and a gray scout with a camera.
- A drone's arms and top plates carry its side's color, in first person and on the battle screen.
- On the battle screen, drones are drawn from the same models and sit in the middle of their cell.

## Fixes

- Tanks no longer read their pictures back from the graphics card while the battle screen is drawn. This addresses Windows battles where soldiers and tanks were missing from the board.

## Compatibility

- Visual changes only. Network protocol **31** and replay schema **3** are unchanged from 0.14.2.4; existing saves and replays remain supported.
