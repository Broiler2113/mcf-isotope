# Isotope 0.14.7

## Ground and plants

- Grass, earth and snow are made from real photographs, the same on the board and in first person, color patches included.
- Grass grows thick on grassy ground: tufts on the board, swaying blades in first person. None on snow. Turn it off in Settings → Graphics → Grass.
- Trees, bushes, hedges, tall grass and potted plants in first person are pictures of real plants on crossed cards. They sway in the wind and frost over in winter.

## First person

- Floors look exactly like the floors on the board.
- Every door looks like its door on the board. Town and field doors swing open on their hinges, away from you; airlocks still slide.
- The Sun gives a lens flare on town and field maps too.
- Overcast skies are smooth.
- Trenches have a low bank of earth along their edges.
- A broken piece of furniture, like half a piano, is closed where it broke instead of see-through.
- Crates and pallets sit on the floor instead of looking sunk into it.
- New setting: Show crosshair.
- After leaving first person, dragging the board with the right mouse button no longer shakes.

## Battle screen

- Bunker, pillbox, armor and corpse walls on the board match their look in first person.
- A tank's name and status show only while your pointer is on the tank.
- Before / After shows the grid the same way the battle does.
- The CRT filter adds a slight color fringe that flares with explosions and near fire.

## Windows

- Close buttons are gone: every window closes with the ✕ in its top-right corner.
- Drag a window by its whole title bar, and resize it from any edge or corner. The Manual too.
