# Isotope 0.14.6.1

## Battle screen

- Clicking one of your units makes it hop up a little. Hovering over units no longer shakes them.
- The buttons in the top-left corner show only their icons. Hover over one to see its name.
- Solar panels hang off every side of a station again, not just the top.

## Settings

- Settings are sorted into pages: General, Graphics, Gameplay, Keybinds and Audio.
- Every slider shows its name on the left and its value on the right.
- New Stars slider: choose how many stars fill space behind the board. It starts at 30%.
- New CRT intensity slider for the CRT terminal filter.
- Defaults resets only the page you are on, and turns dynamic lighting back on instead of off.
