# Isotope 0.11.2

## Fog of war

- Standard fog is now a grey veil over everything you can't see right now. The whole map shows under it: walls, rooms and furniture.
- What changes in the dark stays hidden until you look again: walls built or blown up, new trenches and sandbags, fires. You see the map as you last saw it.
- Units and vehicles outside your sight stay hidden, as before.
- Realistic fog blacks out everything outside your sight: walls, furniture and bodies too.
- Hidden enemies no longer give themselves away: their dice rolls, the details of their shots and their "denied" messages are not shown. When they shoot one of your soldiers you still roll your defence.
- A target marker only shows where you can see the shooter or the target.
- With fog on you can't see the other player's army on the deployment screen. With fog off the "Live placement visibility" option decides, and it now also hides armies that are already ready.

## Drones

- A drone operator can pack up any of your own stations next to him. If there are several, you pick which one.
- A drone sitting on its station is packed up with it. If the drone is out flying, bring it back first.
- Stations in a shuttle seat work properly: they show on top of the shuttle, fly with it smoothly, and can be packed up from the seat.
- A drone hovering over a shuttle can be selected: click once for the drone, again for the passenger, again for the shuttle.
- A drone that can't do anything now says why (for example, its operator walked away from the station).

## Drawing

- The pen and eraser no longer switch off on their own while another player is moving.
- Draw in ten colours. The first swatch is your side's colour.

## Battle

- New setting **Autoroll dice** (Settings → Gameplay): your rolls start on their own, no Roll button or Enter.
- The artillery barrage no longer aims at sleeping civilians, only at soldiers and civilians who have woken up.
- On bunker maps, raiders now break in at the end of a hallway inside the bunker instead of appearing inside the rock.
- Trenches, sandbags and dirt piles are sharp instead of blurry.
- On the heightmap a trench reads -2, and heights are no longer covered by sandbags and dirt piles.

## Multiplayer

- An online game saves itself when a player leaves or the connection drops, so you can carry on later.
- 0.11.2 can only play with 0.11.2: drone stations, the barrage and raiders changed how a match plays out.
