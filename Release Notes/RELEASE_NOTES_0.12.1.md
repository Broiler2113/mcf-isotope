# Isotope 0.12.1

## Small items

- Tables no longer have things painted on them. Mugs, papers, monitors, plates, tools, flasks and more are now separate small items that lie anywhere, at any angle, on tables or on the floor.
- In the map editor, the new **Small items** palette places an item right under the pointer. **R** turns the next one by 15°, and **Erase items** clears them with the brush.
- Every table has an item set (Office, Dining, Coffee, Meeting, Workshop, Lab, Medical, Command or Bare). Placing a table lays its set; the arrows under the brush pick another one.
- Random maps lay their tables to match the room: offices get monitors and papers, kitchens get plates, labs get flasks.
- Explosions throw items off tables like shell casings. Items also fall off furniture that is dragged, picked up or run over, and fire burns them.
- Cabinets, wardrobes, dressers, lockers and filing cabinets open and spill their contents when dragged or blown up.

## Shots land before they kill

- A soldier now falls when the bullet reaches them, not the moment the dice stop. Blood, broken glass and knocked-off items appear with the hit.
- Anti-tank rockets, tank shells and frag grenades fly to where they land. The explosion, the casualties and the wrecked walls come when they arrive.
- The assault shotgun and the machine-gun emplacement now show their bullets.
- When a tank aims, its colored line points at the cell you picked, not at where the shell will fall. As with the anti-tank soldier, you see where it lands when it flies.

## Explosions and fire

- Walls blown apart by explosions throw chunks that look like the wall they came from: brick, concrete, wood, sandbags.
- A pillbox that loses durability spalls chunks out of the side it was hit from.
- Explosions leave soot marks of different shapes on the ground.
- Shell casings, shards, blood, laser scorches and tank tracks are never cleaned up anymore. Everything stays until the end of the battle.
- The flamethrower now fires a jet that runs out from the nozzle. Fire and burned soldiers appear as the flame reaches them.

## Station and asteroid digging

- On station and asteroid maps, soldiers dig into the floor plating instead of the ground. Trenches look like cuts in the deck, and the dug-out piles are heaps of floor scraps and metal called **Rubble Piles**.

## Map editor

- Maps have a time of day: Morning, Day, Evening or Night (Map menu). It sets the lighting in the editor and in battle. The random map generator has the same option, plus Random.
- Light intensity can be typed in as a number, from 10% to 500%. A light set to 300% no longer deletes itself.
- Alternative tiles (furniture colors, door textures, wall accents) are now picked with arrows under the brush, or with the **,** and **.** keys.

## Game modes and maps

- The host can draw the Capture & Hold zones on the map in the lobby (Game Mode → Zone layout → Draw…). Auto stays the default.
- Symmetric random maps place the two deployment zones at opposite ends.

## Art

- New console that joins into long rows, new top-down washing machine, and new station airlocks.
- Changing the color of a glowing object (console, TV, server rack…) now changes the color of its screen or lights too, not just the light it gives off.

## Interface

- The font no longer shows a line through the middle of words, and the zero now has a slash so it can't be mistaken for an eight.
- Hovering over a key on the keybinds keyboard now shows what it does on Mac as well.
- An engineer's Build menu has a **‹ Back** button to return to the unit's actions.

## Multiplayer

- 0.12.1 can only play with 0.12.1.
