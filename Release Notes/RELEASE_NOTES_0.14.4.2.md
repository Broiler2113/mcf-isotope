# Isotope 0.14.4.2

## Performance

- AI turns in big battles run about a third faster: the same 1,200-action giant battle now takes 14 seconds of computer time instead of 23.
- Fog of war updates about three times faster after each action.
- The board is drawn at most once per frame, however many things change in it, so busy moments stutter less.
- AI soldiers pick smoke throws and beam shots faster in crowded battles.

## Compatibility

- Gameplay is unchanged: the same seeded battle plays out move for move as in 0.14.4.1.
- Network protocol **32** and replay schema **3** are unchanged from 0.14.4.1; existing saves and replays remain supported.
