# Isotope 0.14.9

## Battle

- Waves, raider armies and reinforcements always arrive now: bodies, soldiers or vehicles on their landing strip no longer turn them back. They land past them, and further along the edge if they need more room.
- The warning for an incoming wave or raider army outlines the exact cells where it will land, not a small strip at the edge. Under fog of war the outline never gives away hidden soldiers.
- The Aggressor AI is much more aggressive: it closes in harder, never idles in place, worries less about cover and throws grenades sooner.
- Cheat console: `ai pause all` and `ai resume all` pause or resume every AI player at once.

## Battle screen

- Tanks turn smoothly instead of snapping to their new direction, on the board and in first person.
- Wrecked vehicles give off smoke.
- The stationary machine gun (DPMG) looks the same as in first person.
- Walls an engineer builds no longer get colored service stripes.
- The highlight buttons no longer darken the board, the soldier rings are thinner, and you can pan and zoom while the menu is open. Pressing the button again closes the menu.
- The chat's message field is wider and the Send button smaller.
- The Roman numeral badge widens to fit long numbers like XIII.
- Drones count as vehicles in the army counts.

## First person

- Explosions are fireballs that burn out into black smoke, with sparks.
- Smoke and gas are soft drifting clouds instead of gray boxes.
- Wrecks send up columns of smoke.
- The minimap shows the board's real textures.
- Press **R** to remove every roof and ceiling, and again to put them back.
- Other players in first person show their letter, and their name is in the game's font.
- The AI moves at the same speed as on the board.
- Closed doors show their thickness.
- Station roofs no longer show light patches from the lamps inside.
- Wall-high rubble piles on stations and asteroids look like wreckage instead of dirt.
- Drone propellers spin.
- The Earth no longer has stray lines or yellow dots.
