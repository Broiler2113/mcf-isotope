# Isotope 0.14.8

## First person

- Trenches look like they do on the board: plated pits on stations, rock on asteroids, earth on town and field.
- Grass no longer pokes through trench edges.
- Bodies, wall chunks, items and blood in a trench lie at the bottom of it instead of floating at ground level.
- Airlocks next to glass, space or open floor rise into the frame above them instead of vanishing sideways. The frame above a lone airlock matches the station's walls.
- Glass panes in a row join into one sheet, with no lines between them.
- Beyond the edge of town and field maps the ground fades into fog.
- The fog of war is smooth instead of blotchy.
- The Earth is the real Earth, with continents, clouds and city lights.
- The Sun is to scale.
- LDF walls no longer have white spots.

## Battle screen

- Burning cells are filled orange-yellow again.
- Two new buttons at the top left highlight your soldiers or your vehicles: all of them, or only the types you pick (say, only anti-tank and drone operators, or only tanks). The board dims and they glow bright white, even zoomed far out.
- When another player swings their tank's turret, everyone sees it turn, on the board and in first person.
- Bodies floating in space drift like the soldiers there.
- Soldiers killed by explosions leave much more blood and gore.

## Lobby

- A new lobby starts on Annihilation with fog of war off and 5000 points.
- Going back from the purchase screen keeps the lobby's settings.
