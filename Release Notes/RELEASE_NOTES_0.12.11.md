# Isotope 0.12.11

## Combat
- You can now shoot through smoke and gas, but the hit roll needs 2 more. Snipers get no automatic hit through a cloud, and a Marksman's laser loses 1 power per clouded cell.
- A Heavy Infantry soldier standing in its own smoke can now fire at enemies its team can see.
- Nobody fires past a soldier standing in the line, friend or foe. Soldiers leaving a tank no longer shoot through each other.
- Grenades and extinguishers are never thrown past a wall. The AI can't do it either.
- Holdout waves' Shield Bearers survive drone blasts beside them; only a direct hit kills them.
- Waves and raiders land next to fire, never in it.
- Digging a trench never piles dirt on top of a body.
- Heavy Infantry show the "!" mark once they have thrown their smoke grenade.

## Stationary machine guns
- Choose how many of the 8 rounds to fire.
- The rest of the burst can go at other targets without spending more AP.
- The gun turns to face whatever it is firing at.

## Sound
- Every bullet fired is one shot sound, at the weapon's own rate of fire. A second burst no longer plays twice as many shots.
- One click sound for every button, tab, menu, slider and list in the game.
- A tank driving through a wall crunches instead of sounding like an explosion.

## Battle screen
- The line showing who fires at whom appears before the dice again, pointing only at the intended target.
- Raiders now fire from where they stand on screen: each action plays out before they walk on.
- The heightmap shows tanks as height 2.
- Fire is drawn above debris.
- Camera buttons for raider groups and Holdout waves, separate from the players' buttons. Under fog of war, only groups you can see appear.
- After the match, "Show the whole board" turns off the fog of war.

## Performance
- No more stutter when units walk or explosions go off on large, busy maps.
- Undo no longer freezes the screen when the board is full of debris and blood.

## Fixes
- A spectator joining late no longer crashes on the deployment screen.
- Fixed a rare multiplayer desync when a dragged object was destroyed.

**Compatibility:** network protocol 22; all players must use the same release.
