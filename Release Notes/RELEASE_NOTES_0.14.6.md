# Isotope 0.14.6

## Battle screen

- Soldiers and drones twitch when you hover the cursor over them.
- Soldiers, drones and vehicles floating in open space sway gently.
- When a tank aims its cannon, the turret smoothly follows your cursor, as far as the gun can turn.
- The buttons in the top-left corner have icons.
- Walls of stations, towns, fields and asteroids now look like their first-person walls: light hull plates, red brick, concrete block and rock.
- A service's color shows as a band on each side of its walls instead of one line down the middle.
- Fire has a new look from above.
- Dynamic lighting is on by default.
- Large zoomed-out battles run faster: the space backdrop costs a fraction of what it did.

## First person

- Windows are set into their wall, with a sill and lintel of the same material.
- Fire is a glowing flame that lights its surroundings.
- In space you can see the Sun and the Earth, with a lens flare when you look toward the Sun.
- Town and field skies have drifting clouds and a sun.

## Maps and settings

- Station solar panels only appear on the station's top side.
- New **Overcast** option for town and field maps (random map generator and map editor): a gray sky with no sun.
- New **CRT terminal filter** in Settings → Display.

## Compatibility

- Network protocol **33** and replay schema **3** are unchanged: 0.14.5 through 0.14.6 play together, and saves and replays carry over.
